About Us

Strings School of Music was founded with a single vision: to create a sanctuary where raw passion meets world-class professional guidance. Under the expert leadership of our founder, we have grown into Cuddalore's most prestigious academy for both classical and contemporary arts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

What age groups do you teach?

We welcome students of all ages! We have specialized batches for kids (starting from 5 years old), teenagers, and adults. It's never too early or too late to start your musical journey.

Do I need to bring my own instrument?

We provide instruments at the academy for practice during your class. However, we highly recommend purchasing your own instrument so you can practice at home, which is essential for progress.

How long is each class?

Most of our regular music and dance classes are 1 hour long. We also offer extended weekend workshops and intensive training sessions depending on the course you choose.

Can I switch between different dance styles or instruments?

Yes, absolutely! We encourage our students to explore different art forms. You can discuss switching courses with your instructors at the end of your current billing cycle.
